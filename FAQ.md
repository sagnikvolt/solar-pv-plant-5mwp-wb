# Project FAQ

**What is this?** A design and energy-yield study of a 5 MWp ground-mount solar plant, using Kolkata's climate. It is a student project, not a built or bid plant.

**What is the headline result?** 4,994.6 kWp DC, 4 MW AC; 7,078 MWh/yr from PVsyst (PR 85.6%, 1,417 kWh/kWp); 6,917 MWh/yr at the 33 kV point; P90 of 6,458 MWh/yr under an assumed uncertainty budget.

**Is the plot real?** No. The plot and substation are not chosen. The layout is a schematic that fits the 8.0 m row pitch result.

**Why a bifacial module and 8.0 m pitch?** Bifacial gain is about +4.4% in the simulation. The pitch sweep (6.4 to 9.0 m) trades shading loss against land; 8.0 m was adopted (GCR 58%). See `calcs/output/pitch_sweep.md`.

**Why 26 modules per string?** The coldest-day string Voc (1,425 V at 3.4 °C) stays under 1,500 V, and the hot-day Vmp stays above the 600 V inverter minimum. See `calcs/output/string_sizing_design_inputs.md`.

**Are the module and inverter numbers from datasheets?** Yes; they were checked against the manufacturer documents on 28 Oct 2026 (`VERIFICATION.md`). The saved PDFs are not in the repo yet.

**Is the module ALMM/DCR compliant?** Not confirmed for this exact model. Check the MNRE ALMM list before claiming it.

**How reliable are the P90 numbers?** They are only as good as the assumed σ values, which I chose. The independent check is the SAM/PVGIS comparison, which agrees to +0.9% once irradiance is matched.

**Are the costs real?** No. They are indicative ranges (₹3.5 to 5.5 crore/MWp, base 4.4). The CTUIL benchmark of ₹3.5 crore/MW matches the low end. LCOE is not comparable with large-project auction tariffs.

**What is not modelled?** Module-string (electrical) shading, monthly soiling, topography, soil and flood data, grid study, tax and debt. PVsyst also warns about unequal string counts per MPPT input.

**Were the drawings made in AutoCAD?** The geometry is generated by Python (ezdxf). The DXF files were opened and saved as DWG in AutoCAD (education licence); the PDFs and PNGs come from Python.

**How do I reproduce the numbers?** Run the scripts in `calcs/` (each writes to `calcs/output/`) and `python -m unittest discover calcs/tests`. The PVsyst project files are in `PVsyst7.0_Data/`.
